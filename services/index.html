Pest control

Perimeter pest control

The easiest pest to deal with is the one that never gets in. Perimeter pest control puts a treated barrier around the outside of your home to stop pests at the edge and clear them out of the yard.

All of the work happens outdoors, so nothing is sprayed inside your home. We blend our own treatment mix to get the best result for your property.

A good fit if

  • You want to prevent pests, not chase them
  • Bugs keep wandering in from the yard
  • You'd rather keep treatment outside the house

What you get

  • A treated barrier around your home
  • Outdoor-only application
  • Our own blend, adjusted to your property
