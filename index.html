Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you cover?

Coral Springs, Parkland and the surrounding areas of Broward County. If you're just outside those, call us and ask. There's a good chance we can help.

Do you work with businesses too?

Yes. We look after residential and commercial properties, so landlords, property managers and business owners are welcome to get in touch.

Is perimeter pest control done inside my house?

No. Perimeter treatment is applied outdoors, around the outside of your home and in the yard, so nothing is sprayed inside where you live.

Which pests do you handle?

Ants, roaches, spiders, termites, bees, wasps and rodents, plus most other pests you'd find around a Florida home. If you don't see yours here, call and ask.

Is there a discount for veterans?

Yes. Veterans get 10% off. Mention it when you call, or tick the veteran box on the quote form.

When can I reach you?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. We're closed on weekends, but you can send the quote form any time and we'll get back to you as soon as possible. Or call (561) 305-0592.
